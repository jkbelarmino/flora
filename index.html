Corolla & Calyx

The showy petals (corolla) produce vivid visual landing guides for stingless bees and sunbirds, while the outer sepals (calyx) protect young buds against tropical squalls.

Key Example: Mussaenda (Doña Aurora)
Click to Inspect

Velamen & Aerial Roots

Endemic orchids like the Waling-waling lack soil contact; specialized multi-layered spongy velamen tissues rapidly drink moisture from mist and monsoon humidity in high treetops.

Key Example: Vanda sanderiana
Click to Inspect

Cauliflory & Bark Blooms

Flowers sprouting directly from mature woody trunks allow heavy climbing pollinators, giant fruit bats, and wild doves to fertilize blooms under dense rain forest canopies.

Key Example: Katmon Bloom
